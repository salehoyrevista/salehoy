## Long text (400 words)

*Sin motivo* (“for no reason”) is a participatory net art work by Solzimer, ongoing since 29 October 2026 at salehoy.com.ar. It is a dark sky that fills the screen. An address on the Base network receives gifts in ETH or USDC. Each gift becomes a permanent asterisk, from the Greek *asteriskos*, “little star”.

The work asks one question: does anyone give something just because? It is not a donation: it funds nothing and promises nothing. It is not a sale either: the giver gets no object, no certificate, no token. The money goes to the artist, and the work says so. All that remains is the mark that someone answered.

The rules are few. Each asterisk's shape (points, stroke, rotation, size, place) is derived from the transaction's identifier, never from the amount. The amount and the giver are not shown: a cent and a fortune draw the same thing. A gift in ETH can carry a dedication of up to 80 characters, written in the transaction itself. Visits leave trails that fade; gifts stay. The sky turns once per sidereal day, like the real one. Gifts made on the same day form constellations. And the work shows how long it has been since anyone gave.

A gift is usually understood as an exchange: something is given, received and returned. *Sin motivo* removes the return, and also the cause that normally justifies sending money online. What is left is the gesture alone, in plain view.

In art, blockchains have mostly been used as a market: scarcity, ownership, resale. Here the chain is used as a public record. There is nothing to buy and nothing to resell. It does one thing: keep proof, checkable by anyone, that a gift took place. The server verifies every gift directly against the network, with no keys and no paid services, and publishes the state of the sky through a read-only API.

The work belongs to the tradition of net art: not an image uploaded to the web, but something that exists only on the network and with the people who pass through it. The sky is a real-time scene with depth that responds to touch, to the mouse and to the tilt of a phone. It has a generated sound layer, optional, that never starts on its own. Visitors can save their own trail as an image or a video and share it with a link of its own. The site uses no cookies and stores no personal data.

As of 12 November 2026 there are no gifts. The sky is empty. The work does not hide it: it counts the silence. If no one answers, that is an answer too.

## How it works, in 6 points

1. **One gift, one asterisk.** Every gift of native ETH or official USDC sent on Base to the work's address appears in the sky as a permanent asterisk.
2. **The transaction decides the shape.** Points, stroke, rotation, size and place come from its identifier (hash). Neither the artist nor the amount plays a part.
3. **Every gift is worth the same.** The amount and the giver are not shown on the site.
4. **Gifts stay; visits do not.** Whoever passes through the sky leaves a trail that fades. They can save it as an image or a video and share it.
5. **The sky turns and keeps time.** One rotation per sidereal day. Gifts made on the same day form constellations. The work shows how long it has been since anyone gave.
6. **Each gift can carry a dedication.** Up to 80 characters, ETH only, written in the transaction itself. The site does not store it: it reads it from the blockchain.

## Frequently asked questions

**1. What is *Sin motivo*?**
A net art work by Solzimer, ongoing since 29 October 2026. It is a sky: every gift sent on-chain to the work's address becomes an asterisk that stays forever. Visits leave trails that fade.

**2. Is it a donation?**
No. It funds nothing and promises nothing in return. It is a gift, and the work consists of asking whether anyone gives something just because.

**3. Where does the money go?**
To the artist. There is no cause, project or reward. Saying so is part of the work.

**4. How many gifts are there?**
As of 12 November 2026, none. The sky is empty. The current state can be checked on the site, at https://salehoy.com.ar/api/cielo and on the network's public explorer.

**5. Why a blockchain? Is it an NFT?**
It is not an NFT and nothing is for sale. The blockchain is used as a public record: anyone can check that a gift took place without relying on the site or the artist. The giver receives no token.

**6. Can you see how much each person gave?**
On the site, never. Nor who gave it. The asterisk's shape comes from the transaction's identifier, not from the amount. As with any transaction on a public blockchain, the amount and the sending address remain visible on the network.

**7. Who is Solzimer?**
It is the pseudonym of the work's author. No other details are public.

**8. Does the site keep data about visitors?**
It uses no cookies and stores no personal data. It counts visits anonymously: one number per day, per page. Trails are not stored anywhere, unless visitors choose to save their own.
