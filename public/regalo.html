Sin motivo (For no reason)

A net art work by Solzimer. Ongoing since 29 October 2026.

Sin motivo (“for no reason”) is a participatory work by Solzimer, ongoing since 29 October 2026. Every gift sent to its address becomes an asterisk —from the Greek asteriskos, “little star”— that stays in this sky forever. Its shape is decided neither by the author nor by the amount: the transaction decides it. A cent and a fortune draw the same thing. Visits leave trails that fade. It isn't a donation: it funds nothing and promises nothing. It's a question: does anyone give something just because?
